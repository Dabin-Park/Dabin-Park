All four concern the same result — reconstructing the initial functions of the fractional
wave equation from the light cone trace of its solutions, published as
*Reconstruction of an initial function from the solutions of the fractional wave equation on the light cone trace*,
Mathematical Methods in the Applied Sciences **47**(12) (2024), 9469–9475
([doi:10.1002/mma.10078](https://doi.org/10.1002/mma.10078)).
The wording of the title varied from talk to talk; the version actually used on each occasion is:

- **2023-07-ynms** — *Reconstruction of the initial functions from the solutions of the fractional wave equation on the light cone trace*
- **2022-07-grad-workshop** — *Recovering of the initial values from light cone trace of solutions of the fractional wave equation*
- **2022-06-ynms** — *Reconstruction of the initial functions from light cone traces of solutions of the fractional wave equation*
- **2022-05-ksiam-spring** — *Recovering the initial values from the solutions of the fractional wave equation on the light cone trace*
